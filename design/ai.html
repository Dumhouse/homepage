What agents can and can’t do

  • Read the inventory, run scans and health checks
  • Propose hosts, services, links, tags, plugins
  • Recognise products and suggest plugins
  • See passwords or tokens — never
  • Change your servers — Home Services never runs commands on them; it gives the agent the context and fix guides
  • Hard-delete — deletes are soft and need your approval
Every agent action is in the Audit log with its token name.
